Group trips are fun, until the airport is wrong

Group trips are fun until someone ends up at the wrong airport. Two cities, almost the same name, and now somebody is driving two and a half hours to pick up the straggler.

That is one way a trip falls apart. The plan lived in a WhatsApp group chat, an iMessage side chat, two Google Sheets, one for money and one for activities, and an Apple Note that moved to Google Docs because not everyone has an iPhone. Six places. One trip.

Enough about what goes wrong. Here is how TripCircle makes it go right for you and your crew.
